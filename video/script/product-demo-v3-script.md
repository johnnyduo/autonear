Slogan: **Find demand. Build within limits.** Every shot is the live app (demo data where a wallet or vault is needed), recorded frame by frame at 30 fps, 1920x1012 at 1.5x. Voice: Gemini 3.8 flash TTS (Charon), one take per scene. Output: `video/out/autonear-product-demo-v3.mp4` (2:10).

## 1. landing  ·  15.7s

> This is AutoNEAR. It reads public activity on NEAR, finds what people want, and prepares the product to build. Find demand. Build within limits. Let me walk you through it, from the first signal to the last transaction.

## 2. home  ·  12.7s  ·  tag: LIVE APP · DEMO DATA

## 3. watch  ·  11.8s  ·  tag: LIVE APP · DEMO DATA

## 4. council  ·  15.0s  ·  tag: LIVE APP · DEMO DATA

> Then the council. Seven specialist agents, one of them a skeptic, vote on each idea. Let's replay the vote. Treasury rebalancing scores eighty. It needed sixty-two. That is a go.

## 5. build  ·  9.8s  ·  tag: LIVE APP · DEMO DATA

> A go becomes a landing page. The agents write the copy, the layout and the style, and run their checks. You review the page, then you approve it.

## 6. approvals  ·  15.6s  ·  tag: LIVE APP · DEMO DATA

> Anything that costs money or goes live waits here as a card: what it does, what it costs, when it expires. In Manual mode, you hold to approve. In Auto mode, the agent acts by itself, inside your limits.

## 7. vault  ·  13.5s  ·  tag: PUBLIC TESTNET · DEMO DATA

> The limits live in a vault on NEAR: half a NEAR per action, two a day, ten in total, and an expiry date. The blockchain enforces them, not our server. Anything outside the limits is refused.
